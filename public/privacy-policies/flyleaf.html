Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Flyleaf browser extension (hereby referred to as "Extension") created by Abraham Aremu (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


What information does the Extension collect?

Flyleaf does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. It has no analytics, no accounts, and no servers, and it makes no network requests of its own.


How does the Extension use the pages you visit?

To show a page in reader mode, Flyleaf reads the content of the page you are currently viewing — and only after you activate the reader on that page — so it can extract the article text and re-render it in a clean layout. This processing happens entirely on your device. Page content is never sent to the Service Provider or to any third party.


Why does the Extension request access to all sites?

A reader mode has to be able to read whatever page you are on, and that can be any site on the web. The site-access permission is used solely to offer reader mode on the current page; the Extension injects nothing into a page until you open the reader there, and it never transmits page content off your device.


What is stored, and where?

Flyleaf saves your own preferences — such as theme, zoom, line height, which sites you have enabled the reader on, and any per-site previous/next controls you train. This data is stored locally in your browser using the browser's storage (Chrome sync storage), which may sync it across your own signed-in browsers. It is only your settings, it stays within your browser account, and it is never sent to the Service Provider or any third party.


Do third parties see or receive your data?

No. Because the Extension collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere, no data is shared with third parties.


What are my choices?

You can clear the Extension's stored settings at any time from your browser's extension settings, and you can remove all of its data by uninstalling the Extension.


Children

The Extension is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.


Security

Because the Extension does not collect or transmit your information, there is no personal data in transit or held on any server that could be exposed.
